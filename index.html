Welcome! 🐧

Hi, my name is Ben Borne. I'm a product engineer from Carmel, IN and I 3D printed this little penguin. I started this as a fun project with my kids to teach them about app development, and we're treating it like a modern-day Flat Stanley to see how far he can travel just by being passed from person to person.

You don’t need to download anything or make an account. Log his location here, snap a quick picture of the view, and then hand him off to a friend or coworker who might get a kick out of taking him somewhere new.
